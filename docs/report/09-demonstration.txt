File name: 1_OOP_S1_2025-2026_Project
Length: 15 to 20 minutes. Every member introduces themselves and explains their modules.

Workflows shown
  W1  Employee reports an incident -> system suggests category and service, warns about a
      duplicate, calculates priority -> technician assigned -> reporter and technician notified
      (bell updates live, email in Mailpit, push on a phone).
  W2  Technician acknowledges and investigates -> SLA monitored every 30 seconds, warning at 75 %
      and breach alerts to the technician and team lead -> incident resolved -> reporter notified,
      verifies and gives feedback.
  W3  Several services fail together -> duplicates joined or merged -> root-cause analysis on the
      dependency graph ranks the probable common cause (Campus SSO for LMS, ERP Portal and Email)
      with its evidence and blast radius.

Also shown: the three role interfaces, login lockout, a 403 for a wrong role, adding a triage keyword
and switching the assignment strategy without a restart, the Command Centre live feed, the test
notification and delivery log, and the test suite passing.
